Security

Responsible Disclosure

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately so the product and affected customers can be protected.

How to report

Email odreai2025@gmail.com with the subject ODRE PQC Security Report.

What to include

  • Affected product version, operating system and architecture.
  • Concise impact and reproducible steps.
  • Non-secret logs or evidence needed to reproduce the issue.
  • A safe contact method for follow-up.

Keep sensitive material private

Do not post exploit material, customer data, license keys, private keys, passwords or protected downloads in a public issue, repository, forum or social channel. This policy does not promise a bug bounty, response deadline or publication schedule.
