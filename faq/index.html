Frequently Asked Questions

Deployment, evaluation, subscription and support questions.

What is a Unit?

One Unit is one independent licensed production deployment or installation.

Are worker processes separate Units?

Workers within one licensed deployment are not described as separate installations in the current Unit definition. Confirm unusual topologies before purchase.

How are VMs and containers counted?

Each independent installation or production deployment requires a Unit. Confirm autoscaling, cloned, ephemeral or orchestrated designs with ODRE AI.

How does the 14-day evaluation work?

It is a separate product evaluation delivered through the approved activation process and is not a payment-provider billing trial.

What happens if the internet connection is interrupted?

License checks are designed to stay outside each customer request. Exact offline lease and renewal behavior is defined by the delivered product and commercial terms.

Can I move a license?

Transfer or reactivation requires the approved support and device verification process. Do not copy installation state or credentials.

When does access end after cancellation?

Use remains subject to the applicable paid-through period and confirmed license state under the commercial terms.

How do I buy 21 Units or more?

Use Enterprise Licensing for volume terms, deployment planning and procurement review.

Are security updates included?

Active commercial subscriptions include maintenance and security updates for supported versions and environments.

Which platforms are verified?

For v0.2.9: Windows Server 2022 AMD64 and Ubuntu 24.04 LTS AMD64 with Python 3.12.
