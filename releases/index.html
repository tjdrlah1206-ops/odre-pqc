History

Documented development lineage

  • v0.2.8 → v0.2.9F-028-01 parent-object replacement defense and targeted/full re-verification.
  • v0.2.6Native trust, TOCTOU, file-descriptor and inode identity hardening; provider, dependency, and configuration path validation.
  • v0.2.4 → v0.2.5Ubuntu native ABI omission was identified and the missing shutdown export and lifecycle behavior were corrected; later targeted findings kept release blocking in place.
  • v0.1.4Added read-only status observability and re-confirmed the previous security contract.
  • v0.1.3Closed three v0.1.2 findings with strict next-sequence validation and pre-handler rejection of unsupported response contracts.
  • v0.1.2Clean-room attack testing preserved three findings after functional verification: sequence-gap acceptance and two handler-ordering defects.
  • v0.1.1Blocked protected-route plaintext bypass and established fail-closed handler behavior after security failure.
v0.3.0 is the current customer distribution. v0.2.9 remains the sealed Core verification reference; earlier versions are shown only as development and remediation history.
