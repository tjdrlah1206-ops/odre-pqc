Vulnerability Reporting & Responsible Disclosure

Report a suspected security vulnerability privately to odreai2025@gmail.com with the subject ODRE PQC Security Report.

Include the affected product version, operating system and architecture, a concise impact description, reproducible steps, relevant non-secret logs and a safe way to contact you. Do not include customer production data, private keys, license keys, passwords or exploit material in a public issue, public repository or social post.

ODRE AI will review the report and coordinate next steps based on the information available. This page does not promise a specific bounty, response time or disclosure deadline.

Supported Platforms

The current published verified deployment baseline is Windows Server 2022 AMD64 and Ubuntu 24.04 LTS AMD64 with Python 3.12 and FastAPI. “Verified” is reserved for those environments in the v0.2.9 evidence. Other environments are not described as verified.

Data Handling

ODRE PQC runs in the customer application environment. The commercial license service processes the minimum licensing metadata needed to confirm entitlement, activation and maintenance. The current service design does not route customer request bodies, response bodies or PQC plaintext through the licensing service.

License service data scope

  • Commercial entitlement and status information.
  • Purchased plan and Unit quantity.
  • Payment-provider transaction, customer or subscription references needed for verification.
  • Activation and installation metadata required by the approved licensing process.

See the Privacy Policy for website, purchase and license-operation data handling.
