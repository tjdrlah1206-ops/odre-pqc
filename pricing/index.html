Unit definition

What is a Unit?

One Unit means one independent licensed production deployment or installation. The Unit is not a traffic meter and is not described as a separate charge for every worker process inside one licensed deployment.

01

Independent deployments

Separate production installations require separate Units.

02

Workers

Multiple workers within one licensed deployment are not described as separate Units in the current public definition.

03

VMs and containers

Each independent installation or production deployment counts as a Unit. Confirm autoscaling, cloned or ephemeral designs before purchase.

Pricing FAQ

Before you purchase

Are monthly and annual plans recurring subscriptions?

Yes. Monthly plans renew monthly and annual plans renew annually until cancelled under the applicable terms.

What does maintenance include?

Commercial subscriptions include product defect maintenance and security updates for supported product versions, operating systems, cryptographic runtime and Python baseline within the published support policy.

Does cancellation end access immediately?

Use remains subject to the paid-through period and confirmed license state described in the commercial and payment terms.

Is the 14-day evaluation a paid billing trial?

No. It is a separate product evaluation and does not automatically become a paid subscription merely from download.
