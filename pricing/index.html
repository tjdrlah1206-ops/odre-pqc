01

What is a Unit?

Under the current commercial terms, one Unit means one licensed production deployment or installation. The standard range is 1–1,000 Units.

Workers, VMs, and containers

The public terms do not define a separate worker multiplier. If your architecture creates multiple production installations or deployments, or if the boundary between them is unclear, request written quantity confirmation before purchase.

Subscription, renewal, and cancellation

  • BillingMonthly and annual plans are recurring subscriptions billed for the selected Unit quantity.

  • CancellationManage cancellation through the applicable Paddle process or contact license support. The effective end depends on the confirmed subscription state.
