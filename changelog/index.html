ODRE PQCDevelopment log

A public record of what we changed, and why.

ODRE AI is an early-stage vendor. Rather than ask customers and partners to trust us on faith, we keep a dated log of meaningful changes to the ODRE PQC product and to this site. For sealed Core version history and cryptographic evidence, see Release notes and the Trust Center.

2026-09-17

Unified the site's primary brand title

Standardized the primary title across pqc.odreai.com to “ODRE PQC — Self-Managing Security Infrastructure for FastAPI,” replacing inconsistent titles left over from an in-progress redesign (the homepage <title> briefly did not match its own social-sharing tags). “Post-Quantum Security Boundary for FastAPI” is now used consistently as the supporting description of the internal Gateway-to-Core boundary, not as the overall product name.

  • ScopeHomepage and product page title, meta description, Open Graph / Twitter tags, and localized (EN/KO/JA/DE/ES) SEO strings.
  • WhyConsistent, accurate titles across search results, social previews, and every language make it easier for prospective customers to verify who we are and what the product does.
About this log

What belongs here

Entries cover changes worth a customer's attention: naming and positioning corrections, security-relevant fixes, and site-wide consistency work. Routine copy edits and formatting are not itemized individually. Product releases, cryptographic verification, and version history are tracked separately in Release notes, not duplicated here.
